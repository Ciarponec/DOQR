## Description

DOQR is a QR-code digital doorbell that requires no physical hardware.

Create a permanent QR code for your door and place it where visitors can see it. Visitors scan the code and ring without installing an app. DOQR notifies you instantly and lets you manage visitor requests from one place.

KEY FEATURES

• A permanent QR code for each digital doorbell
• Instant visitor and ring notifications
• No app required for visitors
• Text conversations
• Device- or network-level visitor blocking
• Permanent account and data deletion inside the app

FREE PLAN

• 1 digital doorbell and 1 host
• Unlimited ring notifications and text conversations
• Your 3 most recent visits

DOQR PRO

• 3 digital doorbells and up to 3 hosts per doorbell
• 90-day visit history
• Audio and video calls
• Courier notes and delivery code
• 120 audio minutes and 60 video minutes per month under the fair-use allowance

DOQR Pro Annual is an auto-renewable subscription. The App Store shows the current price in your local currency before purchase. You can manage or cancel the subscription from your Apple Account subscriptions and restore purchases inside DOQR.

Audio and video are not recorded. Calls are sent directly over WebRTC or, when required, through an encrypted TURN relay.
