What is deleted?

  • Your DOQR host account and profile information
  • Digital doorbells, QR links, and settings
  • Visit records, messages, courier notes, blocks, and notification tokens associated with the account
  • DOQR subscription verification records

Limited records required for legal obligations, security, or fraud prevention may be retained for as long as necessary and de-identified where possible. Backup copies are removed through the normal backup cycle.

Important subscription information

Deleting your DOQR account does not automatically cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Before deleting the account, cancel DOQR Pro in the store’s Subscriptions section to stop renewal.
